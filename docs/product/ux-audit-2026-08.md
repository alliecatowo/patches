# UX density & action-ownership audit — 2026-08

Status: implemented (fixes below landed in this change) / planned (remaining rows)

Scope: `apps/web` and `apps/tui`, excluding Messages/DM screens, `Thread*`/`ThreadScreen`,
`Profile*`/`ProfileScreen`, and `settings/**` — those surfaces are owned by a concurrent E2EE
workstream. This audit covers #210 (density & action ownership), #213 (FAB preference), and the
copy sweep in #162.

## Method

Swept components/routes reachable from the always-visible chrome (root layout, home feed, page
attribution, search, preferences) for: (a) padding/whitespace disproportionate to content
density, (b) more than ~5 simultaneously visible actions competing for attention on one row, (c)
two affordances that do the same thing, and (d) placement that separates a control from the
content it acts on. Findings are concrete, not aspirational — each cites the file that shows it.

## Non-findings worth recording

Searched for excess whitespace around sparse-content empty states (home/local feed empty
messages, search "type to search" placeholder) — these already collapse to a single short line
with no oversized padding wrapper, so no fix was needed there.

## Out of scope for this pass

Messages/DM screens, Thread screens, Profile screens, and `settings/**` beyond the FAB decision
above were excluded (owned by a concurrent E2EE workstream) and were not audited here. A full
sweep of those surfaces is a follow-up once that work lands.
